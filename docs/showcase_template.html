One AI attacks your warehouse.
Another fixes it. A referee proves it.

LeakHunter finds data leaks the way a real attacker would, closes them with Snowflake-native controls, and then guards the door against the two ways an AI agent can open a new one: a bad change to the warehouse, and a bad package.

Change Firewall

AI agents are getting write access to warehouses. Every change an agent proposes is tested on a zero-copy clone first. LeakHunter attacks the clone, and the change is merged only if nothing leaks. Below: four real submissions, recorded from Snowflake.

What we will not claim
    
      
  • A PASS means not breakable by these attacks, not provably safe.
    • 
      
  • In the demo the pii-guardian skill makes CoCo use the gate. Real enforcement needs a Snowflake-side hook.
    • 
      
  • Account-wide changes (role grants) cannot be tested on a clone, so they always go to a human.
    • 
      
  • Differencing attacks are not built yet.
    • 
      
  • Bouncer catches invented, look-alike, new and vulnerable packages, not a hijacked, long-trusted one.
    • 
      
  • All data is synthetic. Fake SSNs start with 9.
